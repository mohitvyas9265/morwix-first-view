Notification Module — Release 2

Notice enhancements and open questions

Changes agreed in the 10 August discussion, building on the Release 1 notification module. Five items are scoped for R2; four need a decision before they can be scoped.

Status Draft for review
Discussed 10 Aug
Builds on Notification Module R1
Source Meeting notes

Notice lifecycle after this release

Draft → Scheduled → Active → Expired → History

Expiry becomes a real transition rather than an end state: once a notice expires it leaves the working list and lands in History. Statuses and colors are unchanged from R1.

Scoped for R2

  1. Remind the author before a scheduled notice publishes

    A notice sitting in Scheduled should prompt the person who created it ahead of its go-live date, so it can still be edited or cancelled while it is unpublished.

    Open detail
    • How far ahead does the reminder fire — one day, or configurable?
    • Does it go to the author only, or to all notice admins?
    • Channel: in-app, email, or both.
  2. Expired notices move into History

    When a notice passes its expiry date it stops appearing in the active working list and is written to History, where it stays readable. The Expired status is kept so a notice is never silently removed.

  3. A notice that requires a response must carry an expiry date

    Turning on "response required" makes the expiry date mandatory — a notice cannot ask for a response without stating when the response is due. Publishing is blocked until the date is set.

    Open detail
    • Read from the notes as expiry date; confirm whether a time of day is also needed.
    • What happens to a response submitted after expiry — rejected, or accepted and flagged late?
  4. Remove the Recent tab from Overview

    The Recent tab comes out of the Overview screen. Its content is already reachable from the notice list, so the tab was duplicating a view rather than adding one.

  5. Notices becomes a filtered view of OverviewConfirm

    Overview holds the full set of notices, and the Notices view shows a subset of it — the same records under a filter, not a separately maintained list.

    Recorded as agreed but worth restating before build: which filter defines the subset (currently active only, or excluding History) needs pinning down, since it decides whether Notices and Overview can share one data source.

Needs a decision

These four are unresolved. They were raised as questions in the discussion, not agreed as scope. Each needs an owner and an answer before it can be estimated, and items 3 and 4 in particular would change the notice data model.

Nothing below should be treated as committed for R2.

  1. Do notices need templates?Question

    Whether staff compose every notice from scratch or start from reusable templates. Affects whether R2 needs template storage, permissions for who can edit a template, and a template picker in the compose flow.

  2. What are the response types?Question

    "Response required" currently has no defined shape. The set of types — acknowledgement, yes/no, single choice, free text — needs deciding, because it determines the response storage model and how responses are reported back to staff.

  3. Multilingual notices behind a toggleQuestion

    Notices authored in more than one language, shown per recipient, gated behind a toggle so schools that do not need it are unaffected. Open: which languages, whether translation is manual or assisted, and what a reader sees when their language is missing.

  4. Remind parents a day before a notice expiresQuestion

    A reminder to parents the day before expiry, distinct from item 1 in the scoped list, which reminds staff before publishing. Likely only meaningful for notices requiring a response — needs confirming, along with whether parents who have already responded are excluded.

Reading of the source notes

Two readings were inferred rather than stated outright, and are flagged inline above: "expiry data" is taken to mean expiry date, and "notice should be subset of Overview tab" is taken to mean the Notices view filters Overview's records. Correct either before build if the intent was different.
