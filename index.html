One school day with Morwix

Every hour of the day, handled.

Schools don't run on features — they run on a timetable. Here's what Morwix takes off your staff's desk, hour by hour.

07:45

Attendance, from any phone

Teachers mark the register in seconds. Parents of absent students get a message before first period ends — no calls from the office, no paper registers to file.

09:20

Fees that chase themselves

Invoices, reminders and receipts go out automatically. Payments reconcile against each student's account, and the bursar sees exactly who owes what — live.

11:00

Timetables that absorb surprises

A teacher calls in sick. Morwix proposes conflict-free substitutions in two clicks and notifies every affected class, teacher and room.

13:30

Marks entered once, used everywhere

Grades typed into the gradebook become report cards, transcripts and parent updates on their own. Nothing is re-typed, so nothing is mis-typed.

15:10

Buses parents can see

Boarding is logged at the door, and parents follow the route in the app. The gate queue of anxious phone calls disappears.

17:00

The principal's one-page day

Attendance rate, fees collected, incidents logged, tomorrow's cover — one summary at the end of the day instead of five spreadsheets.
